# Harija Labs (Hirajalabs)

> **Building Intelligence. Empowering People.**

Harija Labs is an AI engineering and technology company focused on helping businesses adopt AI, automate operations, and build intelligent software solutions.

---

## 🏢 Business Divisions

### 1. Harija AI – AI Services & Consulting
- **AI Agent Development & Agentic Workflows** (LangGraph, Multi-Agent swarms, Autonomous tool execution)
- **Business Process Automation** (Intelligent document pipelines, ERP/CRM integrations)
- **Enterprise Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** (Vector search, re-ranking, hallucination reduction)
- **Software Engineering** (FastAPI, Python backends, React.js frontends, Microservices)
- **Cloud & Infrastructure** (AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, MLOps & LLMOps)

### 2. Harija Academy – AI Education & Training
- Industry-oriented practical training programs
- Hands-on project implementation and capstones
- Curriculum covering LangChain, LangGraph, RAG, LLM Engineering, Python, and MLOps
